## Pricing Assumptions

1. **Per-seat vs. flat pricing:** Some plans (e.g., GitHub Copilot Business, Claude Team) charge per user. Others (e.g., Cursor Pro, Claude Pro) are flat monthly fees.

2. **Enterprise pricing:** Enterprise plans have custom pricing and are not included in savings calculations. Users on Enterprise plans are assumed to have negotiated rates.

3. **API usage:** For API-based tools (Anthropic API, OpenAI API), users input their total monthly spend. The audit engine doesn't calculate per-token costs.

4. **Minimum seats:** Some plans (e.g., Claude Team) have minimum seat requirements. These are not enforced in the audit engine but are noted in recommendations.

5. **Annual vs. monthly billing:** All prices are monthly. Some tools offer discounts for annual billing, but we use monthly prices for consistency.

## Verification Process

All prices were manually verified by visiting each tool's official pricing page on May 13, 2026. Screenshots were taken for reference (not included in this repo). Prices are subject to change—users should verify current pricing before making decisions.
