## FAQ Section

### 1. Is this really free?

Yes. No credit card, no signup, no catch. We built this to help startups optimize their AI spend. If you want help implementing the recommendations, we can connect you with Credex's team (that's how we make money).

### 2. How accurate is the audit?

The audit uses list pricing from each tool's official website (verified as of May 2026). If you have negotiated enterprise pricing, your actual costs may differ. The recommendations are based on common patterns we've seen across hundreds of startups.

### 3. Do you store my data?

We store audit results (tools, plans, savings) to generate shareable URLs. We don't store personal information unless you choose to share your email for the full report. See our privacy policy for details.

### 4. What if I disagree with a recommendation?

That's fine! The audit is a starting point, not a mandate. Every recommendation includes a reason so you can make an informed decision. If you have context we don't (e.g., specific features you need), trust your judgment.

### 5. Can I use this for my team?

Absolutely. The audit works for teams of any size (1-100+ people). Larger teams tend to find more savings because they're more likely to be on per-seat plans that don't scale efficiently.

## Copy Principles

### 1. Direct, Not Clever
"Stop Guessing" is direct. "Unlock AI Savings Potential" is corporate fluff. Our audience values clarity over cleverness.

### 2. Benefit-Driven Headlines
Every headline answers "what's in it for me?" Not "Our Technology" but "See Exactly Where Your AI Budget Leaks."

### 3. No Jargon
"Audit" is borderline jargon but widely understood. Avoid terms like "optimization engine" or "spend analytics platform."

### 4. Honest, Not Salesy
"No signup, no BS" signals honesty. "Revolutionary AI-powered platform" signals snake oil.

### 5. Startup-Friendly Tone
"Built by Credex for startups serious about efficiency" speaks to the audience. "Enterprise-grade solution" does not.

## A/B Test Ideas

### Headline Variants
- A: "Stop Guessing. See Exactly Where Your AI Budget Leaks."
- B: "Find $500/mo in AI Overspend in 60 Seconds."
- C: "Your AI Tools Are Costing You More Than They Should."

**Hypothesis:** Variant B (specific number) will convert better because it sets a concrete expectation.

### CTA Button Variants
- A: "Audit My Spend (Free)"
- B: "Get My Free Audit"
- C: "Find My Savings"

**Hypothesis:** Variant C (outcome-focused) will convert better because it emphasizes the benefit.

### Subheadline Variants
- A: "Get a personalized audit of your AI tool spend in 60 seconds. No signup, no BS."
- B: "Most startups overspend on AI tools by 30%. See if you're one of them."
- C: "Enter your tools, get instant savings recommendations. Takes 60 seconds."

**Hypothesis:** Variant B (social proof + FOMO) will convert better because it creates urgency.

## Voice & Tone Guidelines

**Voice (Consistent):**
- Direct
- Honest
- Startup-savvy
- No-nonsense

**Tone (Varies by Context):**
- Hero section: Confident, bold
- How it works: Clear, instructional
- FAQ: Friendly, transparent
- CTA: Urgent, action-oriented

**Examples:**

**Good:**
- "Stop guessing where your budget leaks."
- "No signup, no BS."
- "What you do next is up to you."

**Bad:**
- "Leverage our cutting-edge optimization engine."
- "Unlock unprecedented savings potential."
- "Transform your AI spend strategy."

## Conversion Optimization Notes

### Above the Fold
- Headline + subheadline + CTA must be visible without scrolling
- Hero section should take up 60-70% of viewport height
- CTA button should be high contrast (blue on dark background)

### Trust Signals
- "Built by Credex" (brand credibility)
- "No signup required" (removes friction)
- "Trusted by engineering teams" (social proof)

### Friction Reduction
- No email required to see results
- No multi-step forms
- No "book a demo" gatekeeping

### Urgency
- "60 seconds" (time-bound)
- "Stop overspending" (loss aversion)
- "See exactly where" (specificity creates urgency)
